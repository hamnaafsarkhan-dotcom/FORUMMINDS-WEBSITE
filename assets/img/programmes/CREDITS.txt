These are placeholders in the sense that they are stock, not photographs of
a real ForumMinds session. Swapping any of them is a one-line change: drop a
new file in this folder under the same name, or edit the "image" field on the
matching programme in data/programmes.js.

Recommended replacement spec: landscape, at least 960px wide, JPEG, subject
weighted to the left or centre (the panel crops from the right on narrow
screens).
