Common questions

Before you write

How much does a programme cost?

Fees depend on the programme, the format and the number of delegates. Send us the details and we will return a written quotation the same working day.

Can you run a programme that is not listed?

Yes. Over 500 topics sit in the wider catalogue, and we build programmes to order. Describe the capability gap and we will propose an outline.

Do delegates receive a certificate?

Every delegate who completes a programme receives a ForumMinds certificate of completion.

What is the minimum group size for in-house delivery?

In-house delivery usually becomes the cheaper option at around five delegates. Below that, a scheduled session is normally better value.

Can you invoice a company rather than an individual?

Yes — almost all our registrations are invoiced to the employer. Put the billing details in the notes field on the registration form.
