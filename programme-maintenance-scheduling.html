Maintenance Scheduling using Big Data, IoT & Agent-Based Simulation

Overview

Calendar-based maintenance intervenes on equipment that does not need it and misses equipment that does. Condition-based scheduling fixes that in principle. In practice it fails when the data is untrusted, the models are opaque, or the schedule that comes out cannot be resourced.

This programme covers the full path: getting usable condition data off the plant, turning telemetry into a defensible remaining-life estimate, and then using agent-based simulation to test a proposed schedule against resource constraints and failure uncertainty before anyone commits to it.

It is a technical programme aimed at engineers who will build or govern these systems, not at buyers evaluating them.

What you will learn

  • Design an IoT condition monitoring architecture that produces trustworthy data
  • Assess data quality and handle gaps, drift and sensor failure
  • Turn condition data into remaining useful life estimates with stated confidence
  • Build maintenance schedules driven by condition rather than calendar
  • Model resource and shutdown constraints realistically
  • Apply agent-based simulation to test a schedule before committing
  • Interpret simulation output and communicate uncertainty to operations
  • Integrate the result into CMMS and existing planning processes

Who should attend

This programme is designed for practitioners in the following roles.

  • Maintenance and reliability engineers
  • Turnaround and shutdown planners
  • Asset management specialists
  • Condition monitoring teams
  • Instrumentation and control engineers
  • Operations planning leads

Programme agenda

Day 01

Data foundations

  • Condition monitoring architecture: sensors, edge, network, storage
  • What makes plant telemetry trustworthy, and the common failure points
  • Handling gaps, drift, sensor failure and false alarms
  • Big data techniques that suit maintenance datasets
  • Feature engineering from vibration, thermal and process data
  • Workshop: assessing a real condition dataset for fitness
Day 02

From data to decision

  • Degradation modelling and remaining useful life estimation
  • Stating confidence honestly — and what to do when it is low
  • Moving from calendar-based to condition-based intervals
  • Grouping interventions to reduce total downtime
  • Resource, spares and shutdown-window constraints
  • Workshop: building a condition-driven schedule
Day 03

Simulation and deployment

  • Agent-based simulation: what it models that a spreadsheet cannot
  • Building a simulation of your maintenance and resource system
  • Testing a schedule against failure uncertainty and resource limits
  • Reading simulation output without over-trusting it
  • Integration with CMMS and existing planning processes
  • Case exercise: proposing a schedule change and defending it with simulation evidence
